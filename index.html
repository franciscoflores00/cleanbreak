How it works

Three steps. Zero cost to you.

  1. We set up

    We deliver a bank of six flushing units with sinks, rope off a premium zone near your regular restrooms, and staff it for the full event day.

  2. Guests choose

    Attendees pay per visit or grab an all-day Skip the Line wristband. Tap to pay at the door. Nobody is forced in, and the free restrooms stay free.

  3. You share in it

    You pay nothing to host us. We share up to 10% of every sale with you, and your guests leave talking about the restrooms for the right reasons.

Questions

The short answers

What does it cost us?

Nothing. Guests pay us directly for each visit or wristband, and we share up to 10% of those sales with you.

Does this replace our regular restrooms?

No. Your standard restrooms stay free and stay put. Clean Break is an optional premium zone that sits alongside them and takes pressure off their lines.

Who keeps the zone clean?

Our attendants, all day. They clean between rushes, restock supplies, and keep the line moving. The units are pumped and refilled every event day.

Where do you operate?

Southern California to start: Los Angeles, Ventura, Orange County, and the South Bay. Ask us about events farther out.

What do you need from us?

A level spot near foot traffic, truck access for delivery and pickup, and a place on your site map and signage.
